anon@inits ~ % man inits

the fine print.

What's live, what's preview, how the number is counted, and what INITS will never ask you for.

is this financial advice?

No. A receipt shows what already happened. Taking initials at 2x is a rule, not a prediction: on a coin that keeps running, it costs you, and the receipt shows that too.

what's live and what's preview?

Receipts and the 2x watch are live and read real chain and chart data. The auto-sell engine that places the sell for you is preview.

how is "gave back" counted?

Every buy is replayed on its coin's chart. The first candle after the buy whose high reaches 2x that buy's price sells enough to return the SOL you put in. Your own sells are mirrored on what's left. "Gave back" adds up every coin where that beats what you actually got, plus what you still hold at today's price. Coins where you did better yourself are counted separately, so the net is on the card.

why only the last trades and 12 coins?

It runs on free public data with rate limits, so it reads up to 500 recent transactions and prices the 12 coins you put the most SOL into. Fees, priority tips and slippage are in your entry, so the number leans conservative.

do I need to connect a wallet?

No. Paste any address. Never paste a seed phrase or private key anywhere, here or elsewhere.

will inits DM me?

Never. Anyone who does is not us.
